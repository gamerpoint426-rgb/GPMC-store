HOW TO ORDER

Our store uses a Discord-assisted order process. You can build a cart on the website, then share its contents with our team.

01

ADD TO CART

Pick your Diamond, Ascendant or Immortal rank, coins, and tags. Use the category pages to browse.

02

SEND YOUR ORDER

Open your cart and press Send Order to Discord. Copy the summary if Discord does not open a prefilled message.

03

CONFIRM WITH STAFF

Staff will confirm the final price, payment method and delivery details. Never share your Minecraft password.
