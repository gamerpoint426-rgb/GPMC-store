GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Are ranks permanent?

Rank access lasts 35 days: 30 purchased days plus 5 bonus days. Tag purchases are permanent cosmetic unlocks.

How do I receive my purchase?

This version creates an order request. Payment and in-game delivery are manually verified by staff through Discord. Automatic payment and automatic Minecraft delivery are not enabled yet.

Can I use Bedrock?

The store accepts your Minecraft username for order identification. Confirm your exact Bedrock username with staff before ordering if it differs from your Java identity.

Can I get a refund?

Ask staff about the server's refund policy before paying. Digital items may be non-refundable after delivery; publish your final policy before launching the store.
