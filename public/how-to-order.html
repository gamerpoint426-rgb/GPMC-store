How to order

A human checks every payment before anything is delivered.

01

Select items

Choose ranks, coins or tags and add them to your cart.

02

Pay via UPI

Scan the store QR or copy the UPI ID. Pay the exact total.

03

Upload proof

Enter your Minecraft username and UTR, then upload a clear payment screenshot.

04

Staff verification

Your order and screenshot go to the private Discord admin channel for review.

05

Delivery

After an admin approves, the bot runs the configured server command.

Never send your UPI PIN, OTP, bank password or Minecraft password. Screenshots are reviewed manually and do not automatically confirm payment.
